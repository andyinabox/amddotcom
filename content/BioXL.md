I just finished a project called [_Tender Buttons_](https://tenderbuttons.click), an homage to Gertrude Stein's book of the same name. I'm hoping to write more about it sometime soon, but it reminded me of how much I love Markov text generators — even more so in a world full of LLMs.

Some books I've read recently(-ish) that I've enjoyed: [_The Vorrh_](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/16071377-the-vorrh), [_Cryptonomicon_](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/816.Cryptonomicon), and the [_The Making of the Atomic Bomb_](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/16884.The_Making_of_the_Atomic_Bomb). I was impressed with how well _Atomic Bomb_ captured both the excitement of scientific discovery and the horror of the Hiroshima/Nagisaki bombings in the same telling.
