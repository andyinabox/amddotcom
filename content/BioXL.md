
Some recent projects I'm happy with: [Yesterday's News](https://yesterdays.news), which originally started as exhibition piece but morphed into a website; [_Tender Buttons_](https://tenderbuttons.click), an homage to Gertrude Stein's book of the same name. Both of these projects reminded me of how much I love Markov text generators in their more basic (non-LLM) form.

Some books I've read recently(-ish) that I've enjoyed: [_The Employees: a workplace novel of the 22nd century_](https://search.worldcat.org/title/employees-a-workplace-novel-of-the-22nd-century/oclc/1277182477), [_The Vorrh_](https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Vorrh/oclc/906826603), and the [_The Making of the Atomic Bomb_](https://search.worldcat.org/title/17454791). I was impressed with how well _Atomic Bomb_ captured both the excitement of scientific discovery and the horror of the Hiroshima/Nagasaki bombings in the same telling.
